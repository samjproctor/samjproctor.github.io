A digital planner Etsy side hustle is profitable for one reason: planners built for your actual device. Most sellers export iPad-sized PDFs that look wrong on a reMarkable or Kindle Scribe, so calibrating a hyperlinked PDF to your exact screen PPI is the fastest way to stand out. This guide covers the build specs, niche choice, pricing, and listing work that gets a first sale within 30 days.

How much can you realistically earn selling digital planners on Etsy?

Most new digital planner shops earn very little in their first months, and anyone quoting five-figure months is showing you a survivor, not a baseline. A realistic side hustle outcome is a slow ramp: your first sale might take weeks, and steady triple-digit monthly revenue is a genuine milestone, not a starting point. The math matters because Etsy's fees stack up. Payment processing, transaction, and listing fees come off every sale, so a $12 planner nets noticeably less — check the official Etsy seller fee schedule before you price anything. Saturation is real, but calibrated e-ink planners for reMarkable, Supernote, and Kindle Scribe are a thinner field than iPad planners. Build for your own device, document your calibration process openly (we do this on the Notebook Studio blog), and treat earnings as compounding, not instant.

Can you start an Etsy digital planner shop for free?

Yes, down to nearly zero. Etsy charges no listing fee for your first listing, then $0.20 per listing plus a 6.5% transaction fee and payment processing fees on each sale (per Etsy's published fee schedule). Your real cost is time.

Etsy fees you pay before you sell anything

Nothing. Fees only trigger when you list or sell, so a zero-budget launch is possible. Budget roughly $0.20 per listing and ~10% of each sale in combined fees, and price accordingly.

What tools do you need to create a hyperlinked digital planner for iPad?

Vector PDF export is non-negotiable

iPad planner buyers open your file in GoodNotes or Notability and zoom into weekly spreads. Rasterized pages blur at 200% zoom, and reviews mention it. Your tool must export true vector PDFs with every hyperlink intact, so text stays sharp and tabs jump correctly between months, weeks, and index pages. Test the exported file on an actual iPad before listing, because link behavior in the design app does not guarantee link behavior in the exported PDF.

Tool comparison for planner digital Etsy builds

Canva is the fastest start: free tier, drag-and-drop, PDF export with links, but limited control over page order and link precision. Figma gives you precise link anchoring and component reuse, then exports vector PDFs through a plugin workflow. Ep Template Studio is built specifically for planner structures. Notebook Studio (our tool, built for calibrated e-ink screens first) exports vector PDFs with layered hyperlinks and handles device-specific PPI calibration; see the reMarkable planner designer for that workflow. Honest gap: it is tuned for e-ink dimensions, so iPad-first layouts take more manual setup than in Canva.

Whichever tool you pick, study how Etsy surfaces listings before you build, since the Etsy Seller Handbook explains how tags and attributes feed search placement.

How do e-ink planner requirements differ from iPad PDF planners?

reMarkable and Kindle Scribe screen calibration

An iPad planner is built for a 264 PPI retina canvas with color accents. A reMarkable 2 renders 226 PPI in pure greyscale, and the Kindle Scribe sits at 300 PPI. If you export a planner sized for iPad, e-ink devices scale it to fit, and your hyperlinks drift out of alignment with the tap targets. Calibrate the page dimensions to the exact screen resolution of the device, then place link boxes on the underlying PDF layer, not on top of handwriting strokes.

Greyscale, refresh, and layout constraints

E-ink has no color, no backlight, and a slow refresh cycle. This changes design decisions iPad planners never face: dark section headers become muddy grey smudges, dense dotted grids flicker on every page turn, and thin 0.25pt lines can vanish at 226 PPI. Use solid light-grey fills sparingly, keep line weights at 0.5pt or heavier, and avoid full-page background tints that force a full refresh on each navigation.

Building once for both device families

The practical workflow is one design, two exports: a landscape color version for GoodNotes-style apps and a portrait greyscale version calibrated per device. Ep Template Studio handles e-ink templates directly, while Canva and Adobe Express assume a color iPad canvas and need manual greyscale cleanup. Notebook Studio exports calibrated vector PDFs for reMarkable, Supernote, and Scribe dimensions, though it does not yet ship device-specific preview mockups, so test one page on your actual device before building the full planner. Device-specific export presets are documented at https://notebook-studio.com/support.html.

Which planner niches sell best on Etsy in a saturated market?

Etsy's marketplace keeps growing, which means generic 2025 dated planners compete with thousands of near-identical listings. Etsy's own annual report puts active sellers in the millions (Etsy investor materials), so the winning move is to sell where supply is thin: device-specific and habit-specific planners.

Device-specific niches with less competition

Most Etsy planner sellers build for iPad apps like GoodNotes. Far fewer ship calibrated hyperlinked PDFs sized to a reMarkable's 1872×1404 screen or a Kindle Scribe's aspect ratio, so a Supernote- or Kobo Elipsa-specific listing faces a fraction of the competition. Buyers searching "reMarkable planner" or "Scribe hyperlinked PDF" are also pre-qualified: they already own the device and need a template now. Our own Notebook Studio PDF designer exports vector PDFs at exact device PPI, and we see this same gap in our own sales data: device-named listings convert better than "digital planner" listings.

Habit and role-based niches

Habit and role-based planners beat dated ones because they never expire. A ADHD-focused daily rhythm planner, a nursing shift planner, or a teacher's term planner sells year-round with no annual update. We cover layout patterns for this in habit tracker planner layouts for e-ink. Honest limitation: undated niches mean fewer seasonal spikes, so expect slower first-month revenue than a dated planner riding January demand.

How should you price a digital planner as a new Etsy seller?

Price your first hyperlinked planner between $8 and $15. Below $5, e-ink buyers read the tag as a signal of quality: if you won't charge for calibration work, they assume the links and page sizes weren't tested on a real device. Underpricing also traps you, because raising prices on an existing listing costs you reviews and momentum.

Build a ladder instead of a single product. A core planner at one price, a bundle adding dated and undated versions plus device-specific PPI variants at roughly 1.5x, and a premium tier with covers and extra templates at 2x. Bundles raise average order value without a second listing.

One honest note: Notebook Studio prices for the design side, not for selling, so your Etsy fees sit on top of whatever you charge. Check Ep Template Studio and Canva listings for your niche before setting a number, then price at or slightly above the middle, not the floor.

What makes an Etsy digital planner listing rank in search?

Title and tag structure that matches buyer search

Buyers type phrases like "reMarkable planner," "Kindle Scribe digital notebook," and "hyperlinked PDF planner," not "productivity system." Put the device name first in your title, then the format: "reMarkable 2 Digital Planner | Hyperlinked PDF | 1404 x 1872." Use all 13 tags with the same vocabulary: device names, "digital planner," "hyperlinked," "e-ink," and niche terms like "undated." Etsy matches titles and tags against buyer queries, so repeating your keyword in both is the cheapest ranking lever you have. Skip brand names you have no affiliation with in ways that mislead; "compatible with" is safer phrasing.

Mockups that show the device, not just the planner

E-ink buyers want to see the planner on their screen, not an iPad. Your first mockup should show the planner on the actual device, with the screen's proportions correct. The reMarkable 2 display is 1404 x 1872 at 226 PPI, per reMarkable's official specs, so build mockups at that aspect ratio rather than stretching a generic frame. What does a realistic 30-day launch plan look like?

Weeks 1-2: design and calibrate

Pick one niche and one device resolution, then build the template set. If your planner targets reMarkable or Supernote, export a vector PDF calibrated to the exact PPI of the screen; a Kindle Scribe version needs its own page size, so budget a second export pass. Build the hyperlinked index last, once page counts are final, since renumbering breaks every link. Keep the layout simple: a dated core, three index tabs, and one bonus layout beat twenty half-finished spreads.

Weeks 3-4: listing, launch, and iteration

Open the Etsy shop, upload the PDF, and write the title around what buyers actually search: device name plus "digital planner" or "hyperlinked PDF". Mockups should show the planner on the device itself, not a generic tablet frame. Price low enough to earn early reviews, then raise after ten. Post in device-specific subreddits and Discords where you already participate, and treat the first sale as feedback: fix the two most common complaints before adding a second product.

FAQ

Do digital planners still sell on Etsy in 2025 or is the market dead?

The market is crowded but not dead. Etsy counts active sellers in the millions, so generic dated planners face brutal competition. Demand has moved toward device-specific and habit-specific listings. A calibrated reMarkable or Supernote planner targets buyers who already own the hardware and search with the device name, a much thinner field.

Can I sell the same planner PDF for iPad and reMarkable, or do I need separate files?

You need separate files. An iPad planner is built for a 264 PPI color retina canvas, while a reMarkable 2 renders 226 PPI in greyscale. If one file serves both, the e-ink device scales it to fit and your hyperlinks drift away from their tap targets. The workflow is one design, two exports.

What resolution should my planner PDF be for Kindle Scribe?

Calibrate to the Kindle Scribe's 300 PPI screen rather than exporting an iPad-sized page and letting the device scale it. Build page dimensions to match the Scribe's aspect ratio exactly, then place hyperlink boxes on the underlying PDF layer so taps land correctly. Keep line weights at 0.5pt or heavier.

How many listings do I need before my Etsy planner shop gets traction?

One good listing can earn a first sale within 30 days if the title matches what buyers search, but traction compounds with a product ladder. Aim for a core planner, a bundle at roughly 1.5x, and a premium tier at 2x. Three to five listings in one niche and device family beats twenty half-finished spreads.
