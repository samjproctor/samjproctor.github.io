Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on supported tablets, the $6.99 one-time price, e-Ink hyperlinks, and custom schedules.

Does Notebook Studio support my specific e-Ink tablet or iPad? +

Yes. Notebook Studio ships with pixel-exact 1:1 presets and toolbar safe-margin guides for reMarkable Paper Pro (11.8″, 229 PPI), reMarkable Paper Pro Move (7.3″, 264 PPI), reMarkable 2 & reMarkable 1 (10.3″, 226 PPI), Amazon Kindle Scribe (10.2″, 300 PPI), Supernote A5 X2 Manta & A6 X2 Nomad (300 PPI), Onyx BOOX Note Air & Go 10.3, and Apple iPad Pro, Air & mini (for GoodNotes, Notability, and Apple Notes). You can also enter custom pixel or millimeter dimensions for any PDF device.

Is $6.99 worth it if I only need one or two planners? +

A single static PDF planner on Etsy or a physical paper notebook typically costs $10 to $20—and if one section doesn't fit your workflow, you are stuck with it until next year. For a $6.99 one-time purchase (less than one paper planner), you can tweak your layout whenever your routine changes and generate unlimited dated planners for 2026, 2027, and beyond with no subscription or in-app purchases.

Why do my hyperlinks not respond when tapped with a stylus on my e-Ink tablet? +

Most e-Ink tablets separate Stylus Writing Mode from Finger Navigation Mode so you don't accidentally trigger links while handwriting. On reMarkable, Supernote, Kindle Scribe, and BOOX, simply tap navigation tabs and Table of Contents links with your finger rather than the stylus nib.

How do I create an Hourly Schedule table with custom start and end times? +

Select any Table element on the canvas and open the Layout tab in the bottom drawer. Tap Hourly Planner (or 30m Schedule) in the Presets row, toggle 1st Col Times on, and choose your increment (Hourly, 30 mins, or 15 mins), Start Time, and End Time. The table automatically resizes its rows and populates all time labels.

How do I transfer my exported PDF from my iPhone, iPad, or Mac to reMarkable, Kindle Scribe, or Supernote? +

From the Export screen, tap Export / Share PDF and send it straight to the reMarkable Companion App, Amazon Kindle App (Send to Kindle), Supernote Partner App, BOOXDrop, GoodNotes, or save it to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or Dropbox.

Does Notebook Studio collect any personal data or require a subscription? +

No. All notebook design, on-device AI layout generation, and crisp vector PDF compilation happen 100% locally on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac. Notebook Studio does not track you, does not collect personal data, and requires no account sign-up or subscription.
