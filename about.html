About notebook-studio.com

Built for Digital Paper & e-Ink Precision

Notebook Studio is an independent Apple platform software studio founded in 2026 in the United Kingdom, focused on 1:1 calibrated vector PDF stationery tools for e-Ink tablets and iPad.

Why We Built Notebook Studio

Generic PDF planners bought online often render with blurry rasterized lines on high-PPI e-Ink screens and place navigation tabs directly underneath the tablet's system toolbar. Notebook Studio was created so reMarkable, Kindle Scribe, Supernote, BOOX, and iPad owners can design crisp, hyperlinked vector PDF notebooks calibrated to their exact display dimensions and handwriting spacing—right from an iPhone, iPad, or Mac.

Studio Principles

  • One-Time Ownership ($6.99) — No recurring subscriptions, no cloud credits, and no account lock-in.
  • 100% On-Device Processing — Every layout calculation, AI Page Architect structure, and PDF export runs locally on your Apple device.
  • Direct Customer Support — Every support question and device preset request is answered directly at support@notebook-studio.com.
