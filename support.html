Amazon Kindle Scribe

Share the exported PDF directly to the Amazon Kindle iOS app or upload via amazon.com/sendtokindle to preserve all interactive tabs.

Upload via Supernote Partner App, BOOXDrop, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or USB-C transfer.
